Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Rabie Ridge Ink House ("we", "us") operates rabie-ridge-tattoos.co.za from 713 Freedom Drive, Rabie Ridge, Midrand, Gauteng, 1685. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, preferred date, service, placement, your idea and any reference image, when you use the booking or contact forms or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring page, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries, give quotes and manage bookings.

  • To show advertising through Google AdSense (see section 4).

3. Booking and contact forms

Our booking form prepares your details as a WhatsApp message that you send to us yourself. Messages on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can:

  • Manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings (some features may stop working).

5. Sharing your information

We only share information with service providers that help us run the website and advertising (for example Google and our hosting provider), or where the law requires it. These parties may process data outside South Africa.

6. Retention and security

We keep booking and enquiry details only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online transmission is completely secure.

7. Your rights under POPIA

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to its processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

8. Children

Tattoos are only offered to adults (18+), and this website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Our website links to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Google Maps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
