01 THE STUDIO

SERIOUS ABOUT THE ART.

Rabie Ridge Ink House is a tattoo and piercing studio built for people who want quality body art without the unnecessary pressure.

Based on Freedom Drive in Rabie Ridge, Midrand, we create custom tattoos, cover-ups, sleeves, portraits, fine-line work and professional piercings.

Every piece is approached with patience, precision and attention to detail. Whether you already know exactly what you want or you're still developing the idea, we'll help turn it into something you'll be proud to wear.
